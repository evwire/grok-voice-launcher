How it works

One hotkey, one loose sentence. Exact names open instantly on your Mac; anything fuzzy is matched against your own list.

1

Press ⌘ ⇧ Space

Or say “Hey Siri, Voice Launcher” for hands-free. A small box appears and listens.

2

Say it loosely

In any language, mis-transcriptions and all. Exact names and aliases match offline in about 3 ms.

3

It opens, in order

Up to four places per sentence: “open Slack and my dashboard” opens Slack, then your Claude dashboard.

FAQ

Do I need Wispr Flow?

No. Any dictation works (macOS Dictation, Superwhisper, VoiceInk, Spokenly…), and so does typing. Wispr Flow is only used for the optional hands-free mode.

Does it cost per use?

Only fuzzy commands, billed by xAI on your own key. With about 50 destinations that's roughly $0.001–0.004 per command at xAI's list price (Oct 2026). Exact names are free and offline.

Can the AI open something I didn't approve?

No. It may only return ids from your own list. It never produces URLs or shell commands. The worst case is the wrong item from your list.

What data do you collect?

None. There's no server, account or analytics. Your key stays in your Keychain, and fuzzy commands go straight from your Mac to xAI.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 13 or newer, Apple Silicon recommended. Tab switching works with Google Chrome today.

Is it affiliated with xAI or Wispr?

No. VoiceLauncher is independent. It works with their products using your own accounts.
